Kadr — screenshots, annotations, shelves and clipboard history from the menu bar

INSTALL
1. Drag Kadr to the Applications folder (the arrow in the disk window).
2. Launch Kadr from Applications. A viewfinder icon appears in the menu bar.

FIRST LAUNCH
Kadr is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch. Click "Done", then once:
  System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down to the line about Kadr → "Open Anyway" →
  confirm with your password or Touch ID, and launch Kadr again.
After that it opens normally.

On the first screenshot macOS asks for Screen Recording:
  System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording → turn on Kadr, then restart it.
Pasting for you (text shelf, clipboard history) and the three-finger press need Accessibility —
Kadr asks the first time you paste.

HOTKEYS (change them in Settings…)
  ⌥⇧4  capture area (Space switches to window)
  ⌥⇧5  capture window
  ⌥⇧3  capture screen
  ⌥⇧2  capture text (OCR) straight to the clipboard
  ⌥⇧D  new shelf
  ⌥⇧T  text shelf
  ⌥⇧V  clipboard history

Screenshots go to the clipboard and to Pictures/Screenshots.
Editor: arrows, boxes, text, pixelate, counters, crop; ⌘C copies and closes.

CLIPBOARD HISTORY (⌥⇧V)
Everything you copy — text, links, images, files — with search. Just start typing to search;
↩ pastes into the app you were in, ⌘1…⌘9 paste card N. ⌘-click several cards and press ↩
to paste them one after another with Tab in between (login and password into a form).
Passwords are masked; the eye shows them. Filters and Clear History are in the "…" menu.
Press the trackpad with three fingers (a full click) to slide the history in; the same press hides it.
If text is selected in the app, the same press copies it. A soft tick confirms every copy
(turn it off in Settings…).
The history is encrypted and stays on this Mac.

Kadr makes no network connections, collects nothing and never pops up on its own.
